# Day 2 — Prepare to validate the ordering workflow

Prepared a [20-minute interview guide](../owner-interview-guide.md) that starts with a recent order for a perishable item. It covers how the owner chose a quantity, when stock ran out or expired, what the shop recorded, and which constraints affected the order. It also includes a template for capturing observations and linking them to product decisions.

## Evidence and open questions

The shop interview is pending. The [problem statement](../problem-statement.md) describes the proposed workflow, and the following assumptions still need field evidence.

| Assumption to check | Evidence to look for |
|---|---|
| Next-day planning fits the shop's ordering cycle. | A recent order with its cutoff and delivery time. |
| Records can distinguish a stockout from zero demand. | Where the shop records sales and whether it can identify when an item sold out. |
| The owner knows which stock will expire before it can sell. | How the shop records expiry dates and discarded units. |
| A suggested order could fit the owner's routine. | A real limit that changed an order and the information the owner would check before using a suggestion. |
| CSV import is practical. | How the owner currently retrieves sales and stock data, and how long that takes. |
